Privacy Policy

Last updated: Oct 17, 2026

Scribbly is a personal notes app. This page explains what it stores and why.

What we collect

Guest mode: your notes stay in your browser's local storage on your device. Nothing is sent to our server.

Signed in with Google: we store your Google account ID, email address, first name, and a link to your Google profile picture (the image itself stays with Google). We also store your notes: title, text, color, dates and trash status. We never see your Google password.

Cookies and device storage: a session cookie keeps you signed in, for up to 30 days of activity. The app also keeps a copy of your notes and settings in your browser's storage, including offline in the installed app.

How we use it

Only to sign you in, save and sync your notes, and show your name and picture in the app. We use Google user data only for this. We don't sell it, share it for advertising, or run analytics trackers.

Who handles it

Google (sign-in), Render (hosting, which may log technical data such as IP addresses) and MongoDB Atlas (database).

Deleting your data

Go to Settings > Delete account to permanently delete your account and all your notes. Old session records expire on their own. Guest notes can be cleared by clearing your browser data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
